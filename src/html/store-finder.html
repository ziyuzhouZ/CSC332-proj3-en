Location Privacy Statement

We value and protect your privacy. When you use the store locator feature, we ensure that your location data is used only to recommend the nearest stores to you and for no other purpose.

Your location information is processed temporarily on your device only and is not permanently stored on our servers, nor is it shared with third-party organizations or advertisers.

We respect your control over your personal data, and you can manage your location permissions at any time through:

  • Allowing or denying location access permissions
  • Choosing to manually enter your location instead of using automatic location services
  • Clearing your stored location information from your browser at any time
