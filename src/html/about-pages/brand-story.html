Brand Story

2018

Brand Founding

Fashion Store was founded in Shanghai, dedicated to providing high-quality, sustainable fashion clothing for the younger generation.

2019

First Physical Store

Opened the first physical store on Nanjing Road in Shanghai, extending the online experience offline.

2020

Sustainability Initiative

Launched an eco-friendly collection, adopted biodegradable packaging for all products, and established a clothing recycling program.

2022

National Expansion

Opened branches in first-tier cities like Beijing, Guangzhou, and Shenzhen, continuously expanding the brand's influence.

2024

Digital Transformation

Launched a new e-commerce platform to provide consumers with a more convenient shopping experience.

"We believe that fashion is not just about appearance, but a way of life. Through sustainable design concepts and quality products, we hope to bring a unique fashion experience to every customer while taking responsibility for the future of our planet."

— Zhang Ming, Fashion Store Founder
