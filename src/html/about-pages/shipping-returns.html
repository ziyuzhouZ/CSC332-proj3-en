Shipping Information

  • Free shipping for orders over ¥299, ¥10 flat rate for orders below ¥299
  • Supported by SF Express, ZTO Express, YTO Express, and other courier services
  • Remote areas may require additional 2-3 days for delivery
  • Delivery times may be extended during holidays, subject to actual notifications

Returns Policy

Return Conditions

  • 7-day no-reason return (items unworn, unwashed, with tags intact)
  • Quality issues, 15-day return window
  • Returns accepted for items that don't match the order
  • Special items like discounted or custom products are not eligible for no-reason returns

Return Process

  1. Submit return request online, state reason and upload product photos
  2. Customer service will process within 1-2 business days
  3. After approval, return items to provided address
  4. Refund processed within 3-5 business days after warehouse confirmation

Important Notes

  • Returned items must maintain original packaging and tags
  • Return shipping covered by us for quality issues, otherwise by customer
  • SF Express recommended for returns to ensure item safety
